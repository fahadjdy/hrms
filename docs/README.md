# HRMS User Guide

Ye guide un logon ke liye hai jo HRMS roz use karte hain: Company Admin, HR Manager aur Super Admin. Har module ka alag page hai jisme likha hai ki wo module kis kaam ka hai, menu me kahan milega aur kaam step by step kaise karna hai.

## Sabse pehle ye jaan lijiye

- Ye system sirf admin aur HR ke liye hai. **Employees ka koi login nahi hota.** Employee ka saara record HR hi banata aur sambhalta hai.
- Har company ko sirf apna hi data dikhta hai. Dusri company ka data kabhi nahi dikhega.
- Koi bhi employee kabhi delete nahi hota. Jo chhod kar jata hai wo **Past Employees** me chala jata hai aur uska poora record bana rehta hai.
- Salary, borrow aur attendance me jo bhi badlav hota hai uska record rakha jata hai: kisne, kab aur kya badla.
- System phone, tablet aur computer teeno par chalta hai.

## Nayi company: kis kram me kaam karein

Jab company pehli baar system use kare, to is kram me chalna sabse aasan rehta hai:

1. **Company ki settings** bhariye: naam, pata, attendance aur payroll ke niyam. → [Company Settings](13-company-settings.md)
2. **Work shift, weekly off aur holidays** set kijiye. → [Work Shifts aur Holidays](04-work-shifts-aur-holidays.md)
3. **Departments aur designations** banaiye. → [Employees](03-employees.md)
4. **Leave types** banaiye. → [Leave](06-leave.md)
5. **Employees** jodiye, unki salary aur (agar ho to) purana borrow ke saath. → [Employees](03-employees.md)
6. Zarurat ho to **HR users** jodiye aur unka role tay kijiye. → [Company Settings](13-company-settings.md)

## Har mahine ka kaam

1. Roz ya hafte me **attendance** check kijiye aur galti ho to sudhaariye. → [Attendance](05-attendance.md)
2. **Leave requests** approve ya reject kijiye. → [Leave](06-leave.md)
3. Mahine me diye gaye **borrow, overtime, bonus aur deduction** darj kijiye. → [Borrow / Advance](08-borrow-advance.md), [Short Hours aur Overtime](07-short-hours-aur-overtime.md), [Salary, Bonus aur Deduction](09-salary-bonus-deduction.md)
4. Mahina khatam hone par **payroll** banaiye, har employee ka hisaab dekhiye, zarurat ho to adjustment kijiye aur final kijiye. → [Payroll aur Salary Slip](10-payroll-aur-salary-slip.md)
5. **Salary slips** nikaliye. → [Payroll aur Salary Slip](10-payroll-aur-salary-slip.md)
6. Koi employee chhod kar gaya ho to uska **final settlement** kijiye. → [Employee Exit aur Final Settlement](11-employee-exit-aur-final-settlement.md)
